Privacy Policy

SliderBomb

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Anderson Binário ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play SliderBomb (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

SliderBomb is a single-player puzzle game. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no social features. We do not operate any server that receives your data. The only data that leaves your device is handled by the third-party services described in Section 3.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game saves the following on your device only, in the app's private storage. This information is not transmitted to us and is not shared with anyone:

  • Your progress: which levels you have completed and the number of moves used
  • Your settings: language, sound effects, music, vibration, and reminders
  • Your hint balance, and whether you chose the free version or bought the full version
  • A log of your games, used to show progress in the Game and to tune level difficulty: time spent per level, number of moves, undos and restarts, and where a play session ended

None of this identifies you. There is no name, email address, phone number, account identifier, contact list, photo, or location involved. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data, deletes all of it.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

3.1 Advertising (Google AdMob, Android)

On Android, the free version of the Game shows advertisements from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad that grants hints, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads, the AdMob SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (GAID), your IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), technical information about your device and operating system, and your interactions with ads. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the Game asks for your consent before loading ads, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy. You can also reset or turn off the advertising identifier in your Android settings.

Full version. If you buy the full version, the Game stops showing ads: from the next launch after the purchase it no longer starts the AdMob SDK nor asks for ad consent.

3.2 Diagnostics and Analytics (Firebase)

The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics on Android and iOS. Through these services the following is collected automatically:

  • Device model, operating system version, language, country, app version, and session information
  • Crash reports, including the stack trace, the screen and level being played, and a short trail of the screens and levels opened before the crash
  • Gameplay events: screens viewed, levels started and finished (result, duration, moves, undos and restarts), the highest level reached, hints used, tutorials started and completed, and a summary of each play session
  • Monetization events: rewarded ads watched or closed, views of the purchase screen and the choice made (free version, purchase, or back), and completed purchases
  • Feature usage: changes to settings (sound effects, music, vibration, reminders, language), whether notification permission was granted, and when a review request is shown

This data is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name or any account, and it is used solely to fix defects and to understand where the Game is too hard, too easy, or confusing. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it or share it with data brokers.

3.3 In-App Purchases

SliderBomb offers a single one-time purchase, the full version, which removes ads and makes hints unlimited. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never receive, see, or store your payment information — we only receive confirmation that the purchase was completed.

3.4 Reviews and Reminders

The Game may suggest that you rate it using the in-app review of Google Play or the App Store; your rating and comment go directly to that store. Reminders are optional notifications scheduled on your device; you turn them on and off in Settings, and your device asks for your permission first.

4. How We Use Information

  • To save your progress and preferences on your device
  • To show ads in the free version, which keep the Game free
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and improve the Game's difficulty
  • To deliver content you have purchased

We do not use your information for profiling or any automated decision-making that affects you. Ad personalization, where it applies, is handled by Google according to your consent and device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Android) — policies.google.com/privacy; see also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Firebase Crashlytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchases — Payment information is handled entirely by Google or Apple. See policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers, because we do not operate one. Progress and settings remain on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data collected by the third-party services listed above is retained according to their respective policies; Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics data is kept for a limited period defined by Google Firebase.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The free version on Android contains advertisements; the Game has no chat and no user-generated content. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of it.

Because progress and settings are stored only on your device, you can delete all of that data yourself at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. You can review your ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy and reset the advertising identifier in your device settings. For data held by Google AdMob or Firebase, requests can be directed to the services listed in Section 5, or to us at the address below and we will assist.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when a new feature changes what the Game collects. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

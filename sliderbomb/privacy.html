Privacy Policy

SliderBomb

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Anderson Binário ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play SliderBomb (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

SliderBomb is an offline single-player puzzle game. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no social features. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game saves the following on your device only, in the app's private storage. This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not shared with anyone:

  • Your progress: which levels you have completed and which level groups are unlocked
  • Your settings: language, vibration preference, and hint count
  • Whether you have made the one-time in-app purchase
  • Gameplay statistics used to tune level difficulty: time spent per level, number of moves, undos and restarts, and where a play session ended

None of this identifies you. There is no name, email address, phone number, account identifier, contact list, photo, location, or any other personal identifier involved. Uninstalling the Game deletes all of it.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The Game does not display advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers.

3.1 Diagnostics and Analytics

The Game uses, or will use, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Through these services the following may be collected automatically:

  • Device model, operating system version, and language settings
  • App version and session information
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data, including the state of the app at the moment of a crash
  • In-app events, such as level completions and feature usage

This data is pseudonymous and is used solely to fix defects and to understand which parts of the Game need improvement. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it or share it with data brokers.

3.2 In-App Purchases

SliderBomb offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks the full set of levels and makes hints unlimited. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never receive, see, or store your payment information — we only receive confirmation that the purchase was completed.

4. How We Use Information

  • To save your progress and preferences on your device
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and improve the Game's difficulty
  • To deliver content you have purchased

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making that affects you.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchases — Payment information is handled entirely by Google or Apple. See policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers, because we do not operate one. Progress and settings remain on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data retained by the third-party services listed above is governed by their respective retention policies; Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics data is retained for a limited period as defined by Google Firebase.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains no advertising, no chat, and no user-generated content. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of it.

Because progress and settings are stored only on your device, you can delete all of that data yourself at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. For diagnostic and analytics data held by Firebase, requests should be directed to the service listed in Section 5, or to us at the address below and we will assist.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when a new feature changes what the Game collects. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
